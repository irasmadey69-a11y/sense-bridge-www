FAQ

Key questions without legal labyrinth.

Does Sense Bridge give legal advice?

No. Sense Bridge helps you understand a letter and the communication. It does not replace a lawyer.

Does it check suspected fraud or phishing?

Yes. The app helps indicate suspicious elements such as a strange link, time pressure, a request for data or impersonation of an institution. This is risk analysis, not confirmation of authenticity.

Can I use a photo of a letter?

Yes. You can add a photo and the app will try to read the text and prepare an analysis.

Does the website work like an app?

Yes. You can add Sense Bridge to your phone home screen as a PWA shortcut.

How is it different from Google Translate or DeepL?

Sense Bridge does not only translate text. It shows translation, meaning, risks, urgency, suspicious elements and reply examples.
