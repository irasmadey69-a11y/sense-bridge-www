Delete your access data

Sense Bridge does not require a traditional account with a username and password. An email address may be used to check or activate time-based access to the service.

You can request deletion of the access data associated with your email address at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address connected with Sense Bridge.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Sense Bridge data.
  3. Send the request to sensebridge.official@gmail.com.
Send deletion request
We will process a valid deletion request within 30 days.

What will be deleted

  • The email address used to check or activate access.
  • Associated access status and activation information stored for that address.
  • Other server-side records directly linked to that access address, where applicable.

Data stored on your device

Local app data can be removed directly on your device by using the Clear data after analysis option or by clearing the app/site data in your device settings.

Documents and analysis content

Sense Bridge is designed so that uploaded documents and analysis content are used only to provide the requested analysis. They are not intended to be kept as a user document archive.
